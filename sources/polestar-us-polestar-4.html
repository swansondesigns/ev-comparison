More on technology

Included with  every Polestar 4 coupe

  • Polestar includes comprehensive warranties with every Polestar car:

    • Vehicle warranty: covers any parts that fail due to manufacturing defects with free repairs or replacements at an authorized Polestar service point. This warranty covers the first four years after delivery, or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first. It is valid regardless of a change in ownership, provided that the Polestar service programme has been followed.
    • Battery warranty: covers any faults in battery performance or defects in material and workmanship will be resolved free of charge, within 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first. If the battery's State-of-Health (SoH) drops below 70% of its original capacity within the first 8 years of ownership or 100,000 miles, the battery will be replaced or repaired for free.
    • Corrosion warranty: covers the repair or replacement of affected panel(s) perforated by corrosion at an authorized Polestar service point, free of charge. Coverage lasts for the first 12 years after delivery, regardless of a change in ownership, with possible regional variations.
    Read more about warranty

  • Every new Polestar comes with 4 years of free roadside assistance. Pressing the connect button in the overhead console will guarantee 24/7 support, including car recovery.

    Read more on service

  • Regular over-the-air updates are included, enhancing the car's features and systems without the need to visit a workshop. 36 months of Connected Services Plus are also included, enabling web browsing, app use, music streaming, and real-time traffic updates. Drivers can renew this service once the complimentary period expires.

  • Polestar drivers have access to a growing number of public charging stations compatible with the Combined Charging System (CCS) and the North American Charging Standard (NACS). NACS adapters are available for purchase from a Polestar Service Point.

Cradle-to-gate carbon footprint⁶

Polestar 4 coupe Rear motor - 19.8 tCO₂e Polestar 4 coupe Dual motor - 20.7 tCO₂e Cradle-to-gate refers to the environmental impact assessment of a car’s life cycle from the first raw material extraction to it reaching the customer.

Animal welfare-secured Nappa Leather

Sourced by acclaimed leather supplier Bridge of Weir, the leather upholstery for Polestar 4 coupe is fully traceable and adheres to strict animal welfare standards.

Tailored Knit

A made-to-size material, Tailored knit is textile innovation inspired by fashion and footwear. The yarn in this premium upholstery is made from 89% recycled PET.

Renewable electricity

Polestar 4 coupe’s battery cells are produced with renewable electricity, and key aluminium parts come from smelters powered by renewable electricity. These efforts reduce the battery's overall carbon footprint.
