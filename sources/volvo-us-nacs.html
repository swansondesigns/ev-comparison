Charging your Volvo at a Tesla Supercharger

Drivers of compatible fully electric Volvo cars now have access to more than 29,000+ Direct Current (DC) fast chargers on the Tesla Supercharger Network.

NACS Fast Charging Adapter []

Charging at a Tesla Supercharger is easy with the NACS Fast Charging Adapter. Simply connect the adapter to a compatible charger, plug into your vehicle, and enjoy all the benefits when you’re on the road. The NACS Fast Charging adapter can be purchased at your local Volvo Cars retailer.

Find a retailer

Warning: Only use the NACS Fast Charging Adapter for DC fast charging at compatible fast charging stations. Do not use for Level 1 or Level 2 Alternating Current (AC) charging. Not compatible with any AC chargers, including any home or public AC chargers with the North American Charging Standard (NACS) plug type. Read the Adapter Accessories User Guide before use. Improper use could cause severe damage. Only use Volvo Cars-approved adapters.

Finding Stations

Compatible Tesla Supercharger stations can be found using Google Maps built into the vehicle or within the Volvo Cars app. When available, see real time information, including pricing and availability. With Google Maps, Tesla Supercharger stations will be added into your route for fast and efficient charging.

How to Start & Stop a Charge

with the NACS Fast Charging Adapter

Click here for detailed charging instructions.

  1. Open the Volvo Cars app and go to the map symbol .

  2. Press the Tesla Supercharger pin and select the station number you are parked at.

  3. Select a connector.

    If the station requires a NACS fast charging adapter, a badge saying NACS fast charging adapter required is displayed.

  4. Select a payment method and press "Start charging".

  5. Attach the adapter to the Tesla Supercharger cable.

  6. Remove any covers from your car's DC charging port.

  7. Plug the adapter into your car's charging port.

    When the adapter is fully inserted, you will hear a clicking sound as it locks into place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to some of the most common questions.

What is the Tesla Supercharger network?

The Tesla Supercharger network is a DC fast charging network which has more than 29,000 stations that are compatible with Volvo fully electric vehicles.

What is the North American Charging Standard (NACS)?

Also referred to as SAE J3400, NACS is a charging standard that is being adopted across North America.

Can I charge my Volvo plug-in hybrid with a NACS adapter?

No. Volvo plug-in hybrids can only charge via the J1772 standard. The NACS adapter is only intended for DC fast charging.

Can I use my NACS adapter at Tesla Destination Chargers or at Home with a NACS Charger?

No. The NACS adapter is not compatible with Level 1 or Level 2 Alternating Current (AC) chargers, including Tesla Destination Chargers and home chargers with NACS connectors.

Which fully electric Volvo models can access the Tesla Supercharger Network?

The Volvo EX90, EX30, EX40, EC40, XC40 Recharge and C40 Recharge can access the network.

How do I get a Volvo approved NACS adapter?

Adapters will be included as standard equipment with MY2025 EX90, EX40 and EC40.
For existing customers, adapters are available for purchase at your Volvo Cars retailer. Only use an approved adapter for DC fast charging.

How will I find Tesla Supercharger sites?

You will be able to locate compatible Tesla Supercharger sites for your Volvo car in the Volvo Cars app and your cars built-in Google Maps.

How will I know which stations require a NACS fast charging adapter?

In the Volvo Cars app, there will be a ‘NACS fast charging adapter required’ badge for stations requiring a NACS fast charging adapter to charge at the location.

Which Tesla Supercharger generations are compatible?

Generation 3 (V3) and beyond Superchargers will be open to Volvo drivers. Fully electric Volvo drivers will be able to use their Volvo Cars app and in-vehicle Google Maps to locate specific chargers.
