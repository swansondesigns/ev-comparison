    Nissan ARIYA

    No Longer in Production

    2025 was the final year of production in the US for the Nissan ARIYA. Although no longer available, it remains a standout electric crossover, celebrated for its advanced technology, striking design, and exhilarating all-electric performance. If you’re searching for an electrified driving experience, explore the Nissan LEAF—our iconic electric car—or discover other innovative models in our Nissan lineup.

    Nissan ARIYA
    Key Features

    The discontinued Nissan ARIYA is celebrated for its standout performance, offering an EPA-estimated range of up to 289 miles (EVOLVE+ only), All-Wheel Drive system, and going 0–60 mph in as little as 4.8 seconds (select trims only). It remains a memorable example of all-electric innovation.  [[1711]][[1461]][[1514]]

    The Nissan ARIYA was a benchmark for smart connectivity, featuring an intuitive Head-Up Display, customizable gauges, and seamless wireless Apple CarPlay® with Siri® voice control.[[1490]][[48]][[115]][[1668]][[110]][[1189]]

    The Nissan ARIYA delivered a spa-like experience with available heated and cooled front seats, advanced sound isolation, and a Memory System for personalized comfort—setting a new standard for electric vehicle interiors. [[143]]

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the Nissan ARIYA’s driving range?

    The Nissan ARIYA delivered an impressive EPA-estimated range of up to 289 miles on a single charge—enough to handle the daily commute and weekend adventures. [[1711]]

    How powerful was the Nissan ARIYA, and did it offer all-wheel drive?

    The ARIYA delivered 238 horsepower in base form or 389 horsepower with dual-motor AWD. With e-4ORCE all-wheel drive, you got 442 lb-ft of torque and 0–60 in 4.8 seconds—genuine performance paired with confidence in any condition.
    Yes, AWD was available on select trims. The dual-motor setup wasn't just about power; it was about control and traction when it mattered.

    How long does it take to charge a Nissan ARIYA, and is it compatible with Tesla Superchargers (NACS)?

    The ARIYA charged from 10% to 80% in 35–40 minutes on DC fast charging. Level 2 home charging took 8–10 hours for a full charge. It was compatible with Tesla Superchargers via NACS adapter—unlocking access to over 20,000 Tesla Superchargers plus 100,000+ public chargers through the NISSAN ENERGY Charge Network. [[4]][[1731]][[75]]

    How many seats does ARIYA have, and what's the cargo space?

    The ARIYA seated five—enough for your crew. The cabin was lounge-like, spacious, engineered for comfort and versatility and 22.8 cubic feet cargo space —plenty for a weekend getaway or everyday needs. It was designed with SUV practicality in mind, proving that electric didn't mean sacrificing room. [[143]]

    What technology features did the ARIYA include?

    The ARIYA was engineered with precision. Dual 12.3" widescreen displays provided intuitive control with customization. A Head-Up Display kept essential information in your peripheral vision. Wireless Apple CarPlay® with Siri® voice control meant your compatible phone seamlessly integrated into the drive. Over-the-air updates helped ensure your vehicle's technology stayed current. And the power-sliding center console represented thoughtful design—innovation that served a purpose. [[143]] [[1516]]

    What are my alternatives for Nissan ARIYA?

    While we're no longer producing the ARIYA, Nissan's commitment to electric driving continues with the Nissan LEAF and an expanding lineup of electrified vehicles. Both offer excellent efficiency, advanced technology, and the reliability you expect from Nissan. Visit us to explore what's next in your EV journey.
