Volvo Cars unveils America's longest electric range plug-in hybrids: the new XC60 and XC90

Volvo Cars today unveiled its first ever long-range plug-in hybrids, introducing new variants of the best-selling XC60 and XC90 SUVs. The new XC60, which also has a comprehensive design update, delivers class-leading electric range of up to 78 miles on a single charge, with the XC90 providing up to 73 miles. Together, they offer the longest electric range of any plug-in hybrids available in the American market.
