More Reasons to go electric

Tesla Supercharger access is here

The MINI ChargingNetwork continues to expand. With a compatible adapter, MINI EV drivers can now charge at Tesla Supercharger stations—which means it’s never been easier to go the distance.   

Wake up recharged

Begin each day with a full battery when you charge overnight with the standard Flexible Fast Charger or available MINI Wallbox.   

More fun. Less admin.

Since all-electric MINI vehicles require fewer fluids and moving parts, they demand less routine maintenance over their lifespans.

IT PAYS TO GO ELECTRIC.

Not only does making the switch to all-electric cut down on carbon emissions and help protect our planet, but it saves you hundreds on gas and routine maintenance in the process. That’s what we call a win-win.

The amount of time it takes to charge the MINI Countryman SE ALL4 varies depending on the power source.
