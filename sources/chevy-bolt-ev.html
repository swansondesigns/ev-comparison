    Making electric more effective

    Bolt is backed by an 8-yr./100,000-mi.* battery limited warranty. And with its new battery technology, regularly charging to 100% capacity is recommended to achieve the fastest overall charging speeds. Check out the pointers below to help make the most of every mile.

    Charge to 100% 

    Bolt should be regularly charged to 100%, rather than the 80% recommended for most EV batteries. This ensures accurate display of the available range and battery level, optimal performance, and achieving the fastest overall DC Public Fast Charging speeds. 

    One Pedal Driving

    This feature lets you slow down and stop your vehicle using only the accelerator pedal under most conditions,* helping you get the most out of your electric driving range.

    Cabin pre-conditioning

    When you heat or cool your vehicle prior to driving while plugged in, it draws energy from the outlet rather than your battery.

    Relaxed driving

    Smooth driving and gentle acceleration help optimize battery range.

    Watching the weather

    While your EV’s battery is designed to withstand the elements, knowing how temperatures affect charging and performance can be helpful.

    BOLT FAQs

    How do you charge a Chevy Bolt?

    Chevy Bolt can be charged through its standard NACS charge port and the included Dual Level Charger.*

    How long does it take to charge a Chevy Bolt?

    Chevy Bolt takes around 25 minutes to go from a GM-estimated 10% to 80% charge* with DC Public Fast Charging.

    How much is a Chevy Bolt?

    Chevy Bolt is America's most affordable EV* and has a starting MSRP of $28,995.*

    Is the Chevy Bolt discontinued?

    No, the Chevy Bolt is back for a limited time, so get yours today. 

    Can a Chevy Bolt use a Tesla Supercharger?

    Yes, Chevy Bolt can use select Tesla's Supercharges*. The new battery and standard NACS charge port make it possible. Consult the MyChevrolet mobile app* to ensure individual station compatibility.  

    How long do Chevy Bolt batteries last?

    The Chevy Bolt comes with an 8-year/100,000-mile battery limited warranty.* You can help extend your battery’s life by following these simple tips.  

    Where is the Chevy Bolt made?

    The Chevy Bolt is assembled in Fairfax, Kansas.* 

    How much does a Chevy Bolt weigh?

    The Chevy Bolt weighs approximately 3,800 lbs. The weight of the vehicle can vary based on trim and options chosen.

    Do Chevy Bolts have a gas engine?

    No, the Chevy Bolt is a fully electric vehicle.

    Does Chevy Bolt have Adaptive Cruise Control?

    Adaptive Cruise Control* is standard on Bolt. Adaptive Cruise Control allows you to set a cruise control speed and automatically follow a detected vehicle ahead at a driver-selected following gap. If no vehicle is detected in your path, the feature works like regular cruise control. You can always override the feature and take control. 

    Does the Chevy Bolt have a spare tire?

    No, the Chevy Bolt comes with a tire inflator kit. There is no spare tire, which helps keep the overall weight of the vehicle down. 

    Can you tow with a Chevy Bolt?

    No, the Chevy Bolt is not rated to tow.
