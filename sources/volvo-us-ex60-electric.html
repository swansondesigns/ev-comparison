Charging optimized by AI

Breathe Charge software monitors the battery in real time. It learns your patterns and can make fast charging up to 30% quicker, even in cold conditions, without compromising range or long-term performance.

Charging reminders

Get a reminder when the battery drops below 20%. The center display highlights nearby stations in Google Maps with speed, availability and estimated charge time.

Battery pre-conditioning

Navigate to a charging stop and the car automatically warms or cools the battery for faster charging. You can also use the dedicated button to start pre-conditioning anytime.

Convenient home charging

The EX60 comes with a standard 19.2 kWh onboard charger for quick and efficient at-home charging. It’s also prepared for bi-directional charging.

A silhouette that cuts through the air

A silhouette that cuts through the air

When you first encounter the EX60, it feels like the start of a new chapter in electric design. It carries the strong, sculptural presence of an SUV, yet every curve works with the air, not against it.

A low front and gently sloping roof let air flow smoothly over the body, while the raised cabin maintains a confident driving position without increasing drag. Frameless doors and flush glazing keep airflow clean along the sides, and subtle deflectors at the front wheels calm turbulence around the arches. Beneath the car, a flat underbody reduces resistance, while the tapered rear lets air detach cleanly to support range.

Even the smallest detail has a purpose. Active shutters close when cooling isn’t needed, and the mirrors are shaped to smooth airflow. In true Scandinavian form, beauty and efficiency work together to help you go further between charges.

Born-electric architecture

Born-electric architecture

The EX60 is the first Volvo built on our next-generation electric architecture. Every element, from structure to software, is designed to make the car lighter, stronger and more efficient.

Megacasting forms large sections of the body from a single piece of aluminium. It replaces hundreds of smaller parts, cutting complexity and waste while improving strength and precision. Cell-to-body integration builds the battery directly into the car’s structure. It lowers the weight and material usage, boosts range and performance, and frees up more space inside the cabin.

Signature LED lights

Signature LED lights

Thor’s Hammer signature headlights give the car a confident look and clear visibility. The long, wide beam helps you see the road clearly in all conditions. When the car detects approaching road users, Matrix LEDs adapt the light pattern to help avoid dazzling them.

Available high definition pixel headlights offer even sharper visibility. 25,600 tiny light pixels dim precisely around up to five cars and three other objects while keeping the rest of the road bright.

Rear LEDs trace the car’s shoulder line to emphasize its width and continue the distinctive light signature. As you approach or leave, a subtle welcome and farewell sequence activates, with soft ground lighting around the door sills.

Trunk space and tailgate

Does the Volvo EX60 support NACS?

The EX60 will be the first Volvo car to feature a native North American Charging Standard (NACS) charging port, giving EX60 customers direct access to more than 29,000 DC fast chargers on the Tesla Supercharger network without the need for an adapter.
