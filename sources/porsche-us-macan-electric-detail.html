Electrifies the town.

Whether in large cities or up-and-coming towns, the all-electric Macan is at home wherever there is a buzz in the air. Wherever individuality counts. And wherever people have the chance to reinvent themselves.

Macan highlights.

Young. Urban. Electric.

With its progressive interpretation of classic Porsche design, the all-electric Macan shows at first glance exactly what it's made of.

Porsche Digital Interaction.

The new Porsche DI user interface sets new standards with intuitive operation, customizable widgets, and a modern look and feel.

E-Performance.

Typically Porsche: Up to 265 kW (355 hp)¹ and 479 lb-ft of torque¹, high-performance rear-wheel drive and outstanding driving dynamics.

1Details of the measuring method of the specified values can be found at www.porsche.com/gtr21

Personalization.

Pure happiness means something different to everyone. That's why the all-electric Macan can be designed according to your own preferences — with a large selection of customization options and highlights from Porsche Exclusive Manufaktur.

App Center.

Smart infotainment with the App Center bringing a wide range of apps straight to your Porsche. If preferred, you can control it via the AI Voice Pilot – precisely, using natural language, and tailored to the driver and passengers.

Crucial for true individuality: Each and every detail.

Customization.

The optional two-tone interiors in Black/Blackberry¹, Black/Chalk Beige¹, Black/Night Green¹ and Black/Chalk¹ are highlights in themselves. Even more exciting: They can be combined with your preferred paint color. For example, the combination of the leather package in Black/Bramble¹ and the exterior color Provence¹ is particularly eye-catching.

Single speed transmission.

Sporty starting and spirited acceleration — these two typically Porsche attributes also characterize the all-electric Macan. The single speed transmission provides maximum torque in an impressive manner.
415
lb-ft
From the very first few seconds, the all-electric Macan has a powerful 415 lb-ft of torque at its disposal.

Charging performance.

800-volt technology for the shortest possible charging stops: at fast charging stations, the Macan can be charged from 10% SoC to up to 80% SoC in 21.0 min.¹

¹Macan charging time for direct current (DC) with maximum charging power from 10% SoC to up to 80% SoC under optimal conditions (CCS fast charging station with > 270 kW, > 850V, battery temperature 77°F, initial SoC 9% and remaining range < 37 miles).

PASM. 

The electronically controlled Porsche Active Suspension Management (PASM) with 2-valve dampers increases comfort in all driving situations and enables even more precise driving behavior.
