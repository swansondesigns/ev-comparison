FAQs

  • What is the EPA-estimated driving range of the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 by trim?

    The EPA-estimated driving range of the ID.4 varies slightly between trims. 

    • ID.4 Pro: 291 miles⁠
    • ID.4 Pro S: 291 miles⁠ 
    • ID.4 AWD Pro: 263 miles⁠ 
    • ID.4 AWD Pro S: 263 miles⁠  
    • ID.4 AWD Pro S Plus: 263 miles⁠  

    With the function of an SUV, and range that carries, going in an ID.4 just works. 

  • How fast does the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 charge (DC fast charging and home charging)?

    In optimal conditions, using DC fast charging, the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 can charge from 10% to 80% battery in approximately 28 minutes.⁠ This allows for quick top-ups during road trips or long drives.  With home charging, and at up to 11 kW with the available home wall charger, the 2026 Volkswagen all‑electric ID.4 EV typically takes up to 8 hours to go from a completely empty battery to a full charge.⁠

  • Can the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 use Tesla Superchargers? 

    Yes. The 2026 VW ID.4 EV can use Tesla Superchargers.⁠ To enable the ID.4 to use the Tesla Supercharger network, customers must purchase a Volkswagen-approved NACS adapter that will allow their vehicle to charge using the North American Charging System (NACS) fast charger plug.⁠ Access to over 25,000 DC fast chargers is one more way a VW EV helps make life more convenient.

  • What are the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 safety ratings (IIHS and NHTSA)? 

    Here are the safety ratings for the 2026 Volkswagen ID.4 EV from the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS). The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has not yet given its ratings for this vehicle.

    2026 VW ID.4 IIHS Safety Ratings

    Like in previous model years, the IIHS awarded the 2026 ID.4 strong marks across its core crashworthiness assessments.

    • Small Overlap Frontal Crash test (Driver & Passenger Sides): Good (highest rating)
    • Updated Side Impact: Good
    • Front Crash Prevention (pedestrian): Acceptable
    • Headlights for Crash Avoidance: Acceptable to Good, depending on trim/option
    • LATCH Ease of Use (seat belts and child restraints): Good
