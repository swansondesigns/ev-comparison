2026 Lexus RZ Goes on Sale with Upgraded Convenience Features, Expanded Charging Network Options for All Lexus BEV Owners

November 03, 2025
Read More
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share on Linkedin
  • Send email
  • Copy Link
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share on Linkedin
  • Send email
  • Copy Link
  • On sale this month, the 2026 Lexus RZ offers drivers more power, greater efficiency, and dynamic performance, in addition to increased range and shortened charging times
  • More than 25,000 additional NACS charger plugs are available to 2026 RZ owners through the Tesla Supercharger Network, with 2026 RZs featuring Plug & Charge capability
  • Starting this month, model year 2023-25 Lexus RZ drivers will be able to access SAE J3400 NACS plugs via a complimentary adapter available from Lexus dealers
  • All BEVs with Lexus Interface multimedia can enable Apple Maps EV Routing via Apple CarPlay, augmenting route guidance with intuitive, real-time information

PLANO, Texas (Nov. 3, 2025) – Lexus is announcing several initiatives that aim to benefit Lexus RZ battery electric vehicle (BEV) owners, including 2023-25 vehicles and the newly enhanced 2026 models that are arriving at dealers nationwide this month. Lexus RZ drivers will also be able to enjoy access to a vastly expanded network of DC fast chargers, including the Tesla Supercharger Network, new Plug & Charge capability, and intelligent Apple Maps EV Routing.

Expanded Charging Network
Along with being able to use DC fast charging networks like IONNA, ChargePoint, and EVgo, Lexus RZ owners will be able to access more than 25,000 plugs in North America through the Tesla Supercharger Network[1].

Customers can find these Tesla Superchargers, as well as other compatible SAE J3400 North American Charging System (NACS) networks, through the Lexus app.

To make charging even simpler, 2026 model year BEVs will have Plug & Charge, which can initiate charging by simply plugging in after a one-time enrollment setup through the Lexus app.[2]

The Tesla Supercharger Network will be the first charging network that is Plug & Charge-enabled from model year 2026 onward for BEV drivers, with other charging networks such as IONNA, ChargePoint, and EVgo to follow.

“We put our Lexus guests first in everything we do,” said Shawn Domeracki, vice president of Lexus Sales and Dealer Development. “From access to new charging hardware to software-enabled upgrades available for all Lexus BEV drivers, we’re excited to be able to surprise and delight our guests throughout their Lexus ownership experiences.”

Complimentary Charging Adapters
All 2026 Lexus BEVs will come standard with adapters that allow backward compatibility to charge at SAE J1772 and Combined Charging System (CCS) stations, providing even greater convenience and flexibility.

Starting this month, model year 2023-25 Lexus RZ drivers will receive a letter from Lexus directing them to contact their dealer to receive one complimentary NACS charging adapter[3].

Like all Lexus Genuine Accessories, these adapters meet Lexus’s standards for durability and quality and are the only adapter units that come with the assurance of Lexus warranty coverage.

Apple Maps EV Routing
To further complement the BEV ecosystem, Lexus recently introduced support for Apple Maps EV Routing via Apple CarPlay. Available for all 2023 and newer Lexus RZ models, Apple Maps can now access real-time vehicle information to efficiently navigate iPhone users to compatible chargers on the way to their destinations, taking into account factors like battery performance and elevation changes.

To learn more about Apple Maps EV Routing, visit: Apple Maps EV Routing.

Updated January 21, 2026.

[1] MY2023-25 BEV drivers will need a CCS to SAE J3400 adapter (aka “NACS Adapter”) to access NACS plugs.

[2] 4G Network dependent, with an active Remote Connect trial or subscription.

[3] Charging a Toyota or Lexus vehicle with an unauthorized accessory may void the vehicle’s warranty and may negatively impact vehicle performance and safety.

Lexus’ passion for brave design, imaginative technology, and exhilarating performance enables the luxury lifestyle brand to create amazing experiences for its customers. Lexus began its journey in 1989 with two luxury sedans and a commitment to pursue perfection.
