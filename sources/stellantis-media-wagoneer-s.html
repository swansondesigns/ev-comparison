Wagoneer S Newsroom

All-new, All-electric 2025 Jeep® Wagoneer S Limited: Well-equipped, Competitively Priced and Now Open for Orders

February 6, 2025 Hot on the heels of electrifying customers with the arrival of the midsize, all-electric Jeep® Wagoneer S, the Jeep brand is charging ahead with the introduction of the 2025 model-year lineup, with the new Limited trim joining the Launch Edition.

Jeep® Wagoneer S Launches 'Beautiful Things Can Still Get Dirty' Marketing Campaign

December 23, 2024 The campaign, “Beautiful Things Can Still Get Dirty,” will launch on television during Netflix's Christmas Day football games. A 30-second version of the spot will also run across television and the Jeep® brand’s social media channels.

Jeep® Wagoneer S, Ram 1500 Ramcharger Take the Spotlight as Newsweek’s Most Anticipated New Vehicles for 2025

November 13, 2024 The Ram 1500 Ramcharger’s battery-electric vehicle (BEV) technology delivers the industry’s best combination of range, towing and payload. The 2024 Jeep® Wagoneer S Launch Edition is offered exclusively as a BEV with a range of more than 300 miles on a single charge.

Jeep® Wagoneer S Launch Edition Makes First Public Appearance at Rocket Mortgage Classic in Detroit, June 25-30

June 24, 2024 Jeep® Wagoneer S Launch Edition, the Jeep brand’s fully electric global vehicle that will first arrive in the U.S. later this year, will be making its first public appearance during this year’s Rocket Mortgage Classic. 

Wrap it Up! The All-new, All-Electric 2024 Jeep® Wagoneer S Launch Edition Takes Center Stage on Stellantis NA Headquarters in Michigan

June 14, 2024 The Jeep® brand is celebrating the launch of its first global all-electric vehicle arriving in the U.S., the all-new, all-electric 2024 Jeep Wagoneer S Launch Edition, by wrapping an image of the vehicle on the company’s North America headquarters in Michigan.

NYSE Opening Bell Rings to Celebrate All-electric 2024 Jeep® Wagoneer S Launch Edition, Now Open for Customer Reservations

May 31, 2024 Jeep® brand took over the New York Stock Exchange today to celebrate the opening of reservations for the first global Jeep battery-electric vehicle – the all-new, all-electric 2024 Jeep Wagoneer S Launch Edition.

Introducing the All-new, All-electric 2024 Jeep® Wagoneer S

May 30, 2024 Jeep® brand is charging into the electrified SUV segment with the reveal of its first global BEV, the 2024 Jeep Wagoneer S Launch Edition, which will be available in the U.S. and Canada this fall.

Jeep® Wagoneer S Trailhawk Concept Showcases Rugged Off-road Appearance, Legendary 4xe Capability in a Full BEV

May 30, 2024 Jeep® brand is known worldwide for pushing the limits of capability design through its rugged and capable concepts. Easter Jeep Safari and Moab, Utah, have long been a test bed for that creativity. Today, the brand is unveiling of the Jeep Wagoneer S Trailhawk concept.

Jeep® Brand Launches All-new All-electric Jeep Wagoneer S Film 'Surpass Yourself'

May 22, 2024 The Jeep® brand today released “Surpass Yourself,” profiling the all-new, all-electric Jeep Wagoneer S – the brand’s first global BEV, which will launch first in the U.S. and Canada.

First Look Inside the Luxurious All-new, All-electric Jeep® Wagoneer S

January 30, 2024 Today, the Jeep brand shares more details of its first global all-electric SUV, providing a first look at the interior of the all-new Jeep Wagoneer S.

Style, Speed and Sophistication in Motion: Get Ready to Take a Seat in the All-new, All-electric Jeep® Wagoneer S

January 18, 2024 The Jeep® brand is celebrating another year of firsts in 2024, preparing to launch its first global battery-electric vehicle (BEV) and expanding its lineup of legendary SUVs.
