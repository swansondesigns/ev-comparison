FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the range of the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4?

The MINI Countryman SE ALL4 has a range of up to 216 miles.  

How fast does the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4 go from 0-60 mph?

The MINI Countryman SE ALL4 goes from 0-60 mph in 5.4 seconds. ?  

Does the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4 have a Panoramic Moonroof?

Yes, the MINI Countryman SE ALL4 comes standard with a Panoramic Moonroof.

What are the dimensions of the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4?

The MINI Countryman SE ALL4 is 175 inches long and 64.6 inches tall. It’s 72.6 inches wide excluding mirrors, and 81.5 inches wide including mirrors.

What is the towing capacity of the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4?

The MINI Countryman SE ALL4 boasts a towing capacity of up to 2,645 pounds with the available factory-installed trailer hitch.  

What is the battery capacity of the 2027 MINI Countryman SE ALL4?

The MINI Countryman SE ALL4 has a 64.7 kWh battery.
