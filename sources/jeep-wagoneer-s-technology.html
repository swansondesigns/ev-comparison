On Full Display

A 12.3-inch Uconnect® 5 touchscreen allows for immersive access to entertainment and navigation, while the 12.3-inch Digital Cluster Display keeps live vehicle information directly behind the steering wheel for quick-glance access.

Passenger Seat Copilot

The available Front Passenger Interactive Display above the glove compartment lets your second-in-command adjust the navigation, access the vehicle’s cameras or cue up the next hot track.

Vital Stats Up Front

A full-color digital Head-Up Display conveniently projects updated information like speed, incoming call notifications and even GPS directions directly in your line of sight.

Charging Packages

The available Free2move Charge Home Package lets you fully charge your electric vehicle in approximately 9 hours via a 48-amp Level 2 charging station. Our partner, Qmerit can arrange for the required professional installation.

Or choose the available Free2move Charge Go Package, which includes $600 in charging credits to be used at public charging stations around the country.

The Peace of Safety and Security

You deserve protection, no matter who or what is in your vehicle. Explore the incredible safety and security features that offer 2025 Wagoneer S drivers assurance and calm on life’s twisting road.

Learn More About Intelligent Driving Systems

Active Driving Assist

This hands-on-wheel eyes-on-road automation system uses sensors and cameras within your vehicle to center you within your lane and accelerates or applies the brakes as conditions dictate. 

EXPLORE INTELLIGENT DRIVING

Fully Autonomous Parking

Using sensors and contour detection, our Fully Autonomous Parking System guides the Wagoneer S into any space that will fit it.

360° Surround View Camera

Synchronized cameras help monitor busy surroundings and display everything they see on your Uconnect® touchscreen.

ParkSense® Front and Rear Park Assist

Using your vehicle’s camera and sensor array, ParkSense® Front and Rear Park Assist with Active Rear Park Warning can alert you if a front or rear collision might occur during manual parking, and can even stop the vehicle if needed.

Side Distance Warning

Using cameras in the front and rear fascias, this feature offers audible alerts when objects are too close to the sides of the vehicle, even providing visual alerts on the Digital Cluster Display.

Traffic Sign Recognition

Using its in-vehicle cameras, the Wagoneer S recognizes upcoming traffic signs and can warn you if you need to yield or stop.

Active Lane Management

Reading the road as you travel, this feature provides subtle steering wheel input to get you back to the center of your lane if it detects a drift.

Forward Collision Warning with Active Braking

With Forward Collision Warning with Active Braking, radar sensors in the front bumper can detect vehicles and upcoming cyclists in your path—offering audible alerts, applying the brakes and even bringing the vehicle to a complete stop if needed.

Blind Spot Monitoring

Blind Spot Monitoring with High-Speed Overtake Warning sends an alert through your Head-Up Display if a vehicle is in your blind spot, as well as notifying you if a vehicle is coming up fast alongside you.

Drowsy Driver Detection

This system monitors steering wheel behavior using an algorithm that detects fatigue and microsleep and can offer notifications to keep drivers awake and focused.

Intersection Collision Assist

Detecting activity at four-way stops, this feature alerts the driver and even applies the brakes as needed.

Adaptive Cruise Control

Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop and Go automatically adjusts driving speed to maintain a preselected distance from the vehicle ahead, and in certain conditions can bring the Wagoneer S to a full stop.
