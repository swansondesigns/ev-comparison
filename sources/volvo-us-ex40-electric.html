Learn more about the Volvo EX40

Is the Volvo EX40 only available as a fully electric SUV?

The Volvo EX40 is only available as a fully electric car. Not ready to go fully electric? The Volvo XC40 offers the same versatility and style but it’s a gas (mild hybrid) SUV.

What’s the range of the Volvo EX40?

The Volvo EX40 has an estimated range of between 260 mi and 296 mi, depending on your chosen powertrain. To maximize your range and improve charging efficiency, pre-condition the battery before you set off and on your way to charging stations.

*EPA-estimated 118 city /94 highway/ 106 combined for single motor variant. EPA-estimated 103 city/ 85 highway/ 94 combined for twin motor variant. Use for comparison purposes only. Your range and MPGe will vary for many reasons, including but not limited to, driving conditions, how and where you drive, how you maintain your vehicle, battery-package/condition, and other factors. Visit fueleconomy.gov for further information.

Where can I charge my Volvo EX40?

The quickest place to charge is at a DC fast charging station. You can find public charging locations using the Volvo Cars app or Google Maps in regions where charging stations are listed. A Volvo home wallbox lets you charge conveniently overnight. Read more about charging in the electrification hub.

How long does it take to charge my Volvo EX40?

Charging from 10 to 80% takes around 28 minutes at DC fast-charging stations.

This figure is the typical time to charge from 10 to 80 percent at a 200kW DC fast charging station. Figures are preliminary and derived from estimates and calculations performed for the Volvo EX40. These outcomes are not guaranteed. Charging times can vary and depend on factors such as outdoor temperature, battery temperature, charging equipment, battery condition and car condition.

Is the Volvo EX40 a safe car?

The Volvo EX40 meets Volvo Cars’ high safety standards. We’ve included our top standard safety features and innovations in this compact SUV. Learn more about our safety heritage.

Does the Volvo EX40 have Apple CarPlay?

Yes, wired Apple CarPlay is standard in the Volvo EX40. It’s easy to connect your iPhone and access your apps, music and navigation directly through the car’s infotainment system. Apple CarPlay and iPhone are trademarks of Apple Inc. Apple CarPlay is compatible with an iPhone 5 or newer models running iOS 8 or later. Update to the latest software to optimize your Apple CarPlay experience.

Does the Volvo EX40 have Google built-in?

Yes, the Volvo EX40 features Google built-in. Access Google Assistant, Google Maps and Google Play from the center display. Google, Google Play and Google Maps are trademarks of Google LLC.

Is the Volvo EX40 the same as the Volvo XC40 Recharge?

Yes, it's the same car with a new name. Starting for 2025, to streamline our nomenclature, all of our fully electric cars now start with an "E" and no longer include "Recharge".
