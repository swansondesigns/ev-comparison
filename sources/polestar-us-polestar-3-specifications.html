Warranty

4-year vehicle warranty

Repair to or replacement of any part that fails due to faulty materials or manufacturing defects. 

Faults and deviations in the vehicle's paint surface due to a defect in materials or paint application. 

First 4 years of ownership or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first.

8-year battery warranty

Any material defect in the lithium-ion battery pack.

First 8 years of ownership or 160,000 kilometres, whichever comes first.

If the battery’s State-of-Health (SoH) drops below 70% of its original capacity within the first 8 years or 160,000 km (whichever comes first), the battery will be repaired or replaced at no cost.

12-year corrosion warranty

Repair or replacement of the affected panel, if the corrosion is due to faulty materials or construction. 

First 12 years of ownership.

Service and maintenance

Service points

Please contact your Polestar Space for service.

Maintenance

Generally, Polestar 3 doesn't need to be serviced for up to 2 years or 20,000 miles.

Its on-board diagnostics will notify the driver if additional service is necessary, and regularly scheduled over-the-air updates will keep Polestar 3's operating system, apps and car systems as cutting-edge as when they were first released.  

Roadside assistance

Every new Polestar 3 comes with 4 years of complimentary roadside assistance, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

If our technicians can't repair the car at the breakdown location, it will be towed to the nearest service point, free of charge.
