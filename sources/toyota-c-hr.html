Stay in charge.
From home outlets to public chargers: Stay in control of your charge with easy-to-see info inside your C-HR and smart alerts sent right to your devices. Requires account registration on the Toyota app. Certain services require an active Remote Connect trial or subscription. 4G network dependent.
NACS Charging Port C-HR's North American Charging Standard (NACS) port gives you access to thousands of charging stations nationwide—including the Tesla Supercharger Network. With compatibility across most public chargers—including separate adapters for CCS and J1772 chargers—and many integrated into the Toyota app, it’s easy to find stations, start charging and manage payments from one convenient place. Certain services require an active Remote Connect trial or subscription. 4G network dependent.
Dual-Voltage Charging Cable Charge your way with C-HR's included dual-voltage charging cable and connectors, giving you the flexibility to plug into either a standard Level 1 outlet or a Level 2 charger.
Level 2 Home Charging Take advantage of Level 2 charging capability at home. With access to a 240V outlet, you can keep your C-HR ready for wherever you're headed next.
Plug & Charge Capability Simple, secure and designed to keep your day moving. Plug & Charge capability automatically identifies, authenticates and authorizes payment at compatible charging stations without the need for multiple mobile charging applications. Plug & Charge requires an active Remote Connect trial or subscription and account registration on the Toyota app. 4G network dependent.
Charge Scheduling Charge scheduling is designed to help you take advantage of available off-peak electricity rates while plugged in. By using the in-vehicle menus or Toyota app, you can schedule, start and stop charging times. And if you need to charge right away, simply select “Charge Now.” Certain services require an active Remote Connect trial or subscription. 4G network dependent.
Battery Preconditioning C-HR's battery preconditioning system helps optimize its battery temperature for DC Fast Charging out in the cold. Activate battery preconditioning through the multimedia touchscreen or have it start automatically when a DC Fast Charging station is set as your destination with an active Drive Connect trial or subscription and a registered Toyota app account. 4G network dependent.

Level 3: DC Fast Charging

Under ideal conditions, plug in to charge from 10% to 80% in around 30 minutes at a compatible public charging station.

Level 2: 240-Volt

The 11-kW onboard AC charger allows you to take advantage of its Level 2 charging capability to charge your vehicle from 10% to 100% in around seven hours. So plug in at home or at a public station, optimizing your downtime by charging throughout your day or by easily getting a full charge overnight.

Level 1: 120-Volt

A great pick for drivers who don't routinely take long trips. Simply use a common household 3-prong outlet and the included dual-voltage charging cable to top off your charge when you’re not on the go.
