Overview

The latest model to join Kia’s rapidly growing EV family, the EV3 subcompact SUV is positioned below the Kia EV6, but stylistically is more in line with the larger Kia EV9 three-row SUV. It repackages the cute, blocky presence of the EV9 in a much smaller footprint, reimagining the EV9 in a small five-passenger package—but with a slightly softer and friendlier appearance.

Based on the 400-volt E-GMP platform, the EV3 offers two battery options, a choice of front- or all-wheel drive, and a claimed range of up to 320 miles. Inside, a 29.6-inch screen, comprised of a pair of 12.3-inch touchscreens and a five-inch climate control screen, relays all pertinent data. Overall, the EV3 does a convincing job of appearing as the EV9's mini-me.

Expert Tip: Kia killed the Niro EV so the EV3 could live, but there are still plenty of other subcompact EVs to choose from, including the resurrected for 2027 Chevrolet Bolt and Nissan Leaf.

What's New for 2027?

Everything. The EV3 is a new model slated to go on sale in the States in late 2026.

EV Motor, Power, and Performance

  • EV Motors: 201-hp single motor, 261-hp dual motor, 288-hp dual motor
  • Drivetrains: front-wheel-drive, all-wheel-drive.

Kia says the base Light trim uses the smaller 58.3 kWh battery to power the front wheels only. Models with the larger 81.4 kWh battery (standard on the GT-Line and GT, available on the Wind and Land trims) have a two-motor setup with all-wheel drive good for 261 horsepower; the GT, however, gets a boost to 288 horsepower.

Simulated performance audio sounds and faux gear shift software are available to spice things up. The GT gets a specific suspension tune for livelier handling to go with the increased output. A single-pedal driving mode is standard.

Range, Charging, and Battery Life

  • EPA EV Range: 221-321 miles

According to the EPA, the smaller 58.3 kWh battery is good for 221 miles of range. Models equipped with the larger 81.4 kWh battery have a maximum range of up to 321 miles. Kia made extensive use of lightweight body materials, integrated a front bumper active air flap, fitted a full underbody cover, and developed aero-friendly wheels to achieve a range-extending drag coefficient of 0.275 Cd.

Native charging uses a NACS charging port located on the front passenger side, and Kia says the 28.3 kWh battery can be replenished from 10-to-80 percent in 29 minutes; and the larger 81.4 kWh battery can be refilled in 31 minutes. Vehicle to Load (V2L) functionality is standard, allowing users to power devices and charge e-bikes, among other devices. Vehicle to Home (V2H) functionality is also available, allowing the EV3 to send power to the home during power outages. (V2H requires specialized updating of the home's electrical system to facilitate bidirectional operation.)

Fuel Economy and Real-World MPGe

  • EPA City Ratings: 110-131 MPGe
  • EPA Highway Ratings: 96-104 MPGe

The most efficient EV3 models are ones with front-wheel drive and the extended range battery pack. Those models are rated by the EPA for 131 MPGe city, 104 MPGe highway, and 117 MPGe combined.

When we get a chance, we’ll take the EV3 on our highway fuel economy route and update this story with real-world test results. For more information about the EV3’s fuel economy, visit the EPA's website.

Interior, Comfort, and Cargo

First Row: The EV3’s interior design is stylish and contemporary, with smartly integrated tech features and plenty of conveniences. Cloth upholstery is standard but faux leather and suede options are also available. Dual-zone automatic climate control is standard on all models, but you have to upgrade to the Wind trim to get power adjustments for the driver’s seat.

Second Row: Passenger space in the back seat isn’t as generous as in the larger EV9 SUV, but a pair of adults should fit just fine so long as the front-seat occupants aren’t six-footers and can afford to scoot their seats up a little. Air vents on the back of the center console provide good airflow to the back, and the seatbacks can recline or fold in a 60/40 split.

Cargo Capacity: With the rear seats in use, there’s just over 26 cubic feet of cargo space in the EV3’s trunk. Fold the seats forward, and the cargo bay expands to 56.5 cubic feet. There is also a front trunk, but it’s quite small at less than one cubic foot of volume.

Infotainment and Connectivity

  • Infotainment: 12.3-inch touchscreen
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
  • Audio System: 6-speaker stereo (standard), 8-speaker Harmon Kardon stereo (optional)

Similar to the setup in the EV9, two 12.3-inch screens are bisected by a separate five-inch climate control screen, all three positioned under a single panel to create a nearly 30-inch single continuous screen. Infotainment is in the center, the instrument cluster to the left, and climate controls in the middle. Only a few physical controls remain.

Wireless charging and a head-up display are available. Voice recognition is on hand to help drivers access data and apps, and in a somewhat Kubrickian statement, "deepen the human-to-machine interaction over time," according to Kia.

Safety and Driver-Assistance Features

The EV3 comes with several standard driver-assistance features, including systems such as blind-spot monitoring and automated emergency braking. Kia also offers a hands-free driving mode that's bundled with a more advanced version of adaptive cruise control.

Standard Safety Features: front automated emergency braking with pedestrian detection, standard lane-departure warning with lane-keeping assist, standard adaptive cruise control

For more information about the EV3's crash-test results, visit the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) websites.

Warranty and Maintenance Coverage

Kia provides one of the best warranty plans in the industry. Unfortunately, unlike Hyundai, it doesn't come with any complimentary scheduled maintenance.

  • Limited Warranty: five years or 60,000 miles
  • Powertrain Warranty: 10 years or 100,000 miles
  • Complimentary Maintenance: none
