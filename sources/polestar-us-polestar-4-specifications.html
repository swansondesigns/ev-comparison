Warranty

4-year vehicle warranty

The Polestar warranty covers repairs to or replacements of faulty components, the battery pack, paintwork and corrosion caused by manufacturing defects.

The warranty covers the first 4 years after delivery, or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first, regardless of a change in ownership and provided that the Polestar service programme has been followed.

8-year battery warranty

Any faults in battery performance or defects in material and workmanship will be resolved free of charge.

The battery warranty covers the first 8 years of ownership or 160,000 km, whichever comes first.

If the battery’s State-of-Health (SoH) drops below 70% of its original capacity within the first 8 years or 160,000 km (whichever comes first), the battery will be repaired or replaced at no cost.

12-year corrosion warranty

If any part of the bodywork is perforated by corrosion, the affected panel(s) will be repaired or replaced free of charge by an authorized Polestar service point. 

The corrosion warranty covers the first 12 years after delivery, regardless of a change in ownership.

Service and maintenance

Service points

The specialists at our service locations have all the technical expertise a Polestar requires, ensuring that service and maintenance are completed to the highest standards.

Find the nearest service point

Maintenance

Generally, Polestar 4 coupé doesn't need to be serviced for up to 2 years or 30,000 km.

Its on-board diagnostics will notify the driver if additional service is necessary, and regularly scheduled over-the-air updates will keep Polestar 4’s operating system, apps and car systems as cutting-edge as when they were first released.

Polestar assistance

Every new Polestar 4 coupé comes with 4 years of complimentary roadside assistance, available 24/7.

Pressing the Connect button in the overhead console will give access to guaranteed 24/7 support, including car recovery.
