Spacious trunk

Spacious trunk

The trunk of your EX30 can hold up to 12.4 cubic feet. Fold down the rear seat backrests to bump that up to 27.8 cubic feet, including a 2.2-cubic-foot compartment under the trunk floor.

The inner wheel arches are pushed out of the way to widen the trunk and create a flat floor. Lower the trunk floor to stow heavy or bulky items – it’s simple enough to do with one hand. A handy “Will it fit?” diagram is included on the interior trunk lid.

The power-operated tailgate opens with the touch of a button outside the car or on the center display. Depending on equipment level, you can use your key fob, key card, or digital key (depending on device).

What is the difference between EX30 and EX30 Cross Country?

The EX30 Cross Country is a new adventure-focused variant of the EX30 and comes fully equipped with the standard Ultra equiptment level. The adventurous soul of the EX30 Cross Country is brought to life through its unique design, which includes:

•A dark front shield engraved with topographic maps of an Arctic Sweden mountain range •Cross Country badging •0.75-inch increased ride height •Rugged bumpers and sill moldings •Front and rear skid plates •Wheel arch extensions •Standard 19-inch Matte Black Wheels •Available 18-inch Wheels with All-Terrain Tires •Standard Twin Motor Performance AWD powertrain •3.5 second 0-60 MPH acceleration •Available Pine or Indigo upholstery •Up to 227 mile range (EPA estimated)*

Where can I charge my Volvo EX30?

The quickest place to charge is at a DC fast charging station. You can find public charging locations using the Volvo EX30 app or Google Maps in regions where charging stations are listed.

A ChargePoint home wallbox lets you charge conveniently overnight. Read more about charging in the electrification hub.

How long does it take to charge my Volvo EX30?

Charging from 10 to 80 % takes about 26.5 minutes at DC fast-charging stations. Use the center display controls to reach 100 % if you need a full charge to reach your destination or the next charging station.

Does the Volvo EX30 have Apple CarPlay?

Yes, wireless Apple CarPlay comes standard on the Volvo EX30. It’s easy to connect your iPhone and access your apps, music and navigation directly through the car’s infotainment system. Wireless Apple CarPlay is compatible with an iPhone 6 or newer models running iOS 14 or later. Update to the latest software to optimize your Apple CarPlay experience.
